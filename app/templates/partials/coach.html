Step 2 of 3 · your team

These three fight for you

  • Your team is the three stands just below: they fight in every mode. Your best pulls went in on their own.
  • Swap a stand: tap one in your storage, then Add to team. Rarity helps, but levels matter more: stands level up by fighting and by fusing copies.
  • Make them stronger: give them items, and pair stands from the same crew or part for a synergy bonus. The planner tests a team before you fight.
{% elif step == 'story' %}

Step 3 of 3 · the story

Win your first fight

  • Press Fight below, then tap an enemy to attack it. Your stands take turns; specials fire on their own when charged.
  • Every win opens something: the Mirror World after this one, then the dungeon, the daily puzzle and the Tower.
  • Lost later? Home always shows your Beginner's path and the next goal, and Show me around (in your account menu, or Menu on phones) replays the tour.

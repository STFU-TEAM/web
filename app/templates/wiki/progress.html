Mastery, titles & Stand Dex

Long-term goals: get to know your favourite stands, collect whole sets, and wear what you earned under your name.

Stand mastery

Every stand you fight with keeps a record: fights, wins, damage dealt and specials fired. Mastery belongs to the stand, not to one copy, so fusing, releasing or pulling a new copy never resets it. You'll find it in each stand's panel on your Team page, and your best ones show on your profile.

Points: 1 per fight, +3 per win and +1 per special fired. Every fight counts (story, tower, Mirror World, raids, duels…) except the training dummy and fights you surrender.

Titles

A title shows in gold under your name on your profile. Pick which one, or none, at the bottom of your profile. Once earned, a title is yours for good. Ways to earn one:

  • Ranked seasons: finish a month at {{ facts.season_title_from }} or higher, e.g. “Requiem · October 2026” (seasons).
  • Mastery: “<stand> Master” for every stand you master.
  • Stand Dex: complete every stand of a rarity (below).
  • Events: “<event name> veteran” from the event shop.

Stand Dex

The Stand Dex groups stands into sets: every stand of a rarity, and every member of a crew (synergy group). Own each stand of a set once to claim its reward. A stand stays in your Dex for good once you've owned it here, so releasing or fusing a copy, or trading it away, never undoes progress. Each set pays once.

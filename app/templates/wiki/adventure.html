Mirror World, tower & dungeon

Story

Story mode walks you through the game chapter by chapter. Some steps ask you to do something first (build a team, win a fight, claim the daily), and some pay rewards. Progress is shared with the bot's /story.

Mirror World

Energy

Mirror World trips, story and Alternate Universe replays cost energy (the dungeon doesn't). It comes back one point every {{ facts.energy_minutes }} minutes ({{ facts.energy_minutes_donor }} for supporters) until your bar is full; your max grows with your level. A Can of Energy gives {{ facts.energy_can }} at once and can push you past your max, up to {{ facts.energy_bank }}× it. Cans drop from the daily reward and streak, Mirror World, the tower, the boss rush, gang raids and the Crusaders' Journey.

The Mirror World pits your team against a reflection of itself: as many stands as you bring, rarities close to theirs, levels and awakenings scaled from your team. Each trip rolls how sharp the reflection is: Faded (35%, weaker), Clear (40%), Sharp (20%, your equal) or Twisted (5%, stronger, an extra awakening). Harder reflections pay more Meteor Dust and stand XP. A trip costs 1 energy and opens every {{ facts.wormhole_minutes }} minutes.

Tower

The tower never ends. A climb costs {{ facts.tower_cost }} Meteor Dust and starts at floor 1; the same three fighters climb on, so health and growth carry from floor to floor, with a small heal after each one and a rest stop every 5 floors that also revives the fallen. Enemies level up fast (level 100 by floor 27), gain an awakening at floors 10, 18, 26, 34 and 42, get a little stronger every floor, and past floor 42 that growth compounds (about ×2 health and damage ten floors later); every 10th floor has a boss, and a loss ends the climb. The floors and the leaderboard reset every Monday; each floor pays out the first time you clear it that week.

Dungeon

The dungeon is free: no energy, no Dust, one run a day. Every day brings a new dungeon, the same for everyone: three floors of corridors under a fog, each hiding monster packs, an elite, chests, traps and a campfire. Your team walks in at full health and keeps its wounds from fight to fight; campfires heal and get the fallen back up, traps hurt. Enemies are matched to your team's level and get tougher on each floor.

Fights, elites and chests fill a loot bag that's only paid when you leave. On each floor's stairs, take the bag home or go deeper. The third floor's stairs are guarded by a boss that adds crafting materials and sometimes a Devil's Palm. If your team falls, or you give up, only half the bag comes home. Stand XP from each fight is yours either way.

Modes

Wormhole, tower & dungeon

Story

Story mode walks you through the game chapter by chapter. Some steps ask you to do something first (build a team, win a fight, claim the daily), and some pay rewards. Progress is shared with the bot's /story.

Wormhole

The wormhole throws you at a random team scaled to your level. Win for fragments, XP and sometimes an item. It cools down for {{ facts.wormhole_hours }} h ({{ facts.wormhole_hours_donor|int }} h for supporters).

Tower

The tower is a climb through fixed floors that get tougher every time you clear it. A climb costs {{ facts.tower_cost }} fragments, and your floor is saved between climbs.

Dungeon

The dungeon is a grid you explore by wagering energy. Tiles hide fights, chests and bombs. Reach the ladder to clear it.

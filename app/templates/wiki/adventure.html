Wormhole, tower & dungeon

Story

Story mode walks you through the game chapter by chapter. Some steps ask you to do something first (build a team, win a fight, claim the daily), and some pay rewards. Progress is shared with the bot's /story.

Wormhole

The wormhole throws you at a random team scaled to your level. Win for fragments, XP and sometimes an item. It cools down for {{ facts.wormhole_hours }} h ({{ facts.wormhole_hours_donor|int }} h for supporters).

Tower

The tower never ends. A climb costs {{ facts.tower_cost }} fragments and starts at floor 1; the same three fighters climb on, so health and growth carry from floor to floor, with a small heal after each one and a rest stop every 5 floors that also revives the fallen. Enemies grow exponentially, every 10th floor has a boss, and a loss ends the climb. The floors and the leaderboard reset every Monday; each floor pays out the first time you clear it that week.

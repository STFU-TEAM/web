Story

Story mode walks you through the game chapter by chapter. Some steps ask you to do something first (build a team, win a fight, claim the daily), and some pay rewards. Progress is shared with the bot's /story.

Mirror World

Energy

Mirror World trips, story and Alternate Universe replays and the dungeon cost energy. It comes back one point every {{ facts.energy_minutes }} minutes ({{ facts.energy_minutes_donor }} for supporters) until your bar is full; your max grows with your level. A Can of Energy gives {{ facts.energy_can }} at once and can push you past your max, up to {{ facts.energy_bank }}× it. Cans drop from the daily reward and streak, Mirror World, the tower, the boss rush, gang raids and the Crusaders' Journey.

The Mirror World pits your team against a reflection of itself: as many stands as you bring, rarities close to theirs, levels and awakenings scaled from your team. Each trip rolls how sharp the reflection is: Faded (35%, weaker), Clear (40%), Sharp (20%, your equal) or Twisted (5%, stronger, an extra awakening). Harder reflections pay more Meteor Dust and stand XP. A trip costs 1 energy and opens every {{ facts.wormhole_minutes }} minutes.

Tower

The tower never ends. A climb costs {{ facts.tower_cost }} Meteor Dust and starts at floor 1; the same three fighters climb on, so health and growth carry from floor to floor, with a small heal after each one and a rest stop every 5 floors that also revives the fallen. Enemies level up steadily (level 100 around floor 40), gain an awakening at floors 15, 25, 35, 50 and 60, then grow stronger every floor; every 10th floor has a boss, and a loss ends the climb. The floors and the leaderboard reset every Monday; each floor pays out the first time you clear it that week.

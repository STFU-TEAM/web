Gangs & player shops

Gangs

Gangs are the clan system, shared with the bot. Founding one costs {{ facts.gang_cost|num }} fragments. Bosses and capos invite players by username or Discord ID.

Ranks

  • Boss: promotes, demotes, pays from the vault, hands out stash items and edits the gang profile. Promoting a capo hands them the boss seat.
  • Capo: invites and kicks soldiers, starts wars and raids, recalls guardians.
  • Soldier: deposits, adds guardians and attacks.

Guardians and wars

Members lend up to 3 stands from storage as guardians. A capo queues a war; once matched, it lasts 2 days. Each member attacks the enemy guardians once; your score is the share of guardians you knock out. The gang with more damage per member wins elo and 10,000 fragments for the vault.

Raids

A capo spends the raid cost from the vault to start a 24-hour raid on a boss team. Each member attacks once; damage adds up. Reach the target and every attacker earns fragments and stand XP, and the gang stash gets a rare item part.

Wars are matched and raids are closed by the bot's background workers, so they need the bot running.

Player shops

Open a player shop for {{ facts.shop_cost|num }} fragments, list items at your own price, and buy from other players.

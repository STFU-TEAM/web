Items & equipment

Every item in the game: what it does and every place it comes from. Drop chances are read from the same tables the game rolls, so they're always current.

Equipping

Gear adds health, damage, speed, critical or armor. Each team stand holds up to 3. Equip and unequip them from the items page or from a stand's panel on the team page. Some gear has its own special that charges during fights. Usable items are spent from the items page; materials go into crafting. Spare items sell for a tenth of their shop price; free drops can't be sold but can be traded.

How gear scales

An item's numbers grow with the stand's stars: each ★ adds a third, so at ★5 an item gives 2.67× what it says. Then the stand's types apply on top (a Health type multiplies the health an item gives, a Luck type adds its crit to the item's). Gear also powers specials: every special scales with one stat (shown on each stand's page), and lifting that stat above the stand's natural value makes the special stronger. Luck specials gain about 1.2% per crit point, speed specials 2% per speed point, and health specials grow with the share of health added.

That opens off-meta builds. A revenge tank like Wonder of U wants health (its special returns 30% of the health it has lost) and luck (its special scales with crit): Calamity Charm, Rokakaka Branch and Iggy's Collar to draw the hits. Speed and luck strikers want Valkyrie's Horseshoe, armored crit stands want Mista's Hat.

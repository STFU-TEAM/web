Items & equipment

Equipping

Items add health, damage, speed, critical or armor. Each team stand holds up to 3. Equip and unequip them from the items page or from a stand's panel on the team page. Some items have their own special that charges during fights.

Where items come from

  • The daily reward (60% chance)
  • Mirror World wins and dungeon chests
  • The shop and player shops
  • Crafting from recipes on the items page. Materials (Golden Ratio Shard, Rokakaka Fruit, Devil's Palm Sand) drop from gang raids, the boss rush, the wormhole and the daily reward. They craft gear like the Steel Ball, Rokakaka Graft and Ultimate Being Mask, and Devil's Palm Sand rebuilds Devil's Palms and Requiem Arrows.

Selling

Sell spare items from the items page for a tenth of their shop price. Free drops like chips and raid parts can't be sold, but you can trade them.

Consumables

Items that can't be equipped, like Devil's Palms and bags of Meteor Dust, are used straight from the items page.

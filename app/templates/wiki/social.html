Community

Friends, gifts & trading

Play with other people: add friends, send them gifts, keep up through the inbox, and swap or sell stands.

Friends

Find players by name, then send a friend request from their row or profile. You can have up to {{ facts.max_friends }} friends. Friends can watch each other's friendly duels, and you can challenge, trade with or gift any of them in one tap from Friends.

Invite links

Your Friends page has a personal invite link. Anyone who signs up with it becomes your friend right away. Once they clear {{ facts.gift_stage }} story stages, your “Bring a Friend” quest counts them and pays a Devil's Palm (and “Squad Goals” at 3 friends).

The gift lands on their Friends page and in their inbox; they open everything at once with Open all. Gifted energy can push you past your max, like a Can of Energy. Limits keep it fair:

  • one gift per friend per day;
  • each player receives at most {{ facts.gift_cap }} gifts a day;
  • you need to clear {{ facts.gift_stage }} story stages before you can send any.

Inbox & notifications

The 🔔 bell opens your inbox: duel challenges, trade offers, friend requests and gang invites waiting on you, then a feed of everything that happened. Its badge counts unread news, open requests and unopened gifts. Most news also pops up as a toast on your next page.

You hear about: duels and challenges, trade offers, auction bids and sales, a stand coming home from the Crusaders' Journey, gang invites and war results, gifts, friend requests, quests, achievements and season rewards. Everything stays on the site.

Trades

Open a trade with any player (⇄ Trade on their profile or row). Each side can put in up to {{ facts.trade_stands }} stands, items and Meteor Dust. Nothing moves until the other player accepts, and ownership is checked again at that moment, so nobody can be cheated. Offers expire after {{ facts.trade_days }} days, and you can have {{ facts.trade_open }} open at a time. Locked stands can't be traded.

Auction house

Sell stands on the auction house, up to {{ facts.auction_max }} listings at a time. A listed stand leaves your collection until it sells or comes back to you; its items return to your inventory.

  • Fixed price: in Meteor Dust, Arrowheads or both. The first buyer gets it. Listings last {{ facts.auction_days }} days.
  • Bids: pick a currency, a starting bid and a length ({{ facts.auction_hours|join(', ') }} hours), plus an optional buyout. Each bid must beat the last by {{ facts.auction_raise }}%. Your bid is held right away and returned the moment someone outbids you. A bid in the last {{ facts.auction_snipe }} minutes pushes the end back, so nobody wins by sniping.

The house keeps {{ facts.auction_fee }}% of the Meteor Dust from a sale; Arrowheads go to the seller whole. You can cancel a listing until the first bid. An unsold stand comes back when time runs out.

🔎 Looking for: post the stand you want (with a minimum level or awakening if you like) and what you'd pay, up to {{ facts.wanted_max }} requests at a time. Your offer is held from your wallet right away, so anyone who owns a match can sell it to you in one tap and is paid on the spot (minus the same {{ facts.auction_fee }}% on the Meteor Dust); the stand's gear stays with the seller. A request nobody fills is refunded after {{ facts.wanted_days }} days, or when you cancel it.

Chat

The chat has a global channel, private messages, your gang's channel and each co-op raid's. Type :name: or use 😊 for emoji, emotes and stickers. An automod keeps it clean: slurs, floods and repeated messages are stopped, links to other sites stay out of the global chat, and messages that look like a scam are blocked (in private messages they arrive with a warning) and reported to an admin. New accounts can only start a few new conversations a day. Report anything with ⚑; block a player from your conversation with them.

🛡️ Nobody from the game will ever ask for your password, and the trade and auction pages are the only safe way to swap stands: if someone asks you to "send first", block and report them.

Accounts

Log in with Discord (the same save as the bot) or a username and password. You can also play as a guest: no sign-up, the save lives in a cookie on that browser. Save it with a username from your account page whenever you like: until then guests can't chat, trade or use the market. Players with a username can change their name once every {{ facts.rename_days }} days.

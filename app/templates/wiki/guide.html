Progression guide

The whole path from your first pull to Over Heaven, in the order it opens. The story drives everything; past Part 3, each new part, universe and act also asks for a player level, so the rest of the game (quests, Mirror World, raids) is part of the climb, not a detour.

5 · The endgame

  • Over Heaven opens with the end of the story: {{ g.tracks|length }} tracks of puzzle fights where a team built for each fight's rule beats raw rarity. Start with {{ g.tracks[0].title }}, which teaches the rules one at a time. ({% for t in g.tracks %}{{ t.title }} · {{ t.fights }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %})
  • Over Heaven co-op raids open at the same time: the weekly raid boss with a rule, for 2 or 3 players.
  • Heaven's Gate opens once Requiem · Over Heaven is cleared: four fights at full Requiem strength under Over Heaven's rules, each built around an impossible power. The World stops time every third round, King Crimson erases your turns while Epitaph shows you who his stands will strike, Heaven's Door hides your enemies' health until you read it, and Made in Heaven shrinks your turn clock every round. Before each fight you pick a pact: a blessing with a curse attached. Clearing all four earns the title “Heaven's Gate”.
  • Training ground opens once the story and every Alternate Universe chapter are cleared: level any stand fast and earn chips.

Stuck on a fight?

  • Read the line under the enemies: it says what the fight asks (level, stars, gear) and what your team is missing.
  • Run it in the team simulator first (the 🧪 Simulate link), or try other stands and gear in the team planner.
  • Level your team and give it stars (upgrading stands): short on copies, buy them with Stand Discs. Equip and refine items, and socket chips.
  • Look for a synergy: a team that shares a crew or a part hits much harder (explorer).
  • Replay cleared stages for stand XP while you wait for a level gate.

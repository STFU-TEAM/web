Progression guide

The whole path from your first pull to Over Heaven, in the order it opens. The story drives everything; past Part 3, each new part, universe and act also asks for a player level, so the rest of the game (quests, Mirror World, raids) is part of the climb, not a detour.

1 · Your first day

  1. Pull. You start with an Arrowhead: spend it on a banner for ten stands.
  2. Set your team. Put your best three in your team. A team from one crew or part lights a synergy (explorer).
  3. Start the story. {%- if g.parts[0].opens %} Your first win opens {{ g.parts[0].opens|join(', ') }}.{% endif %} {%- if g.from_start %} {{ g.from_start|join(', ') }} {{ 'is' if g.from_start|length == 1 else 'are' }} open from the start.{% endif %}
  4. Claim your daily and check quests: they're your steadiest income.

“What if the villains won?” Each chapter opens after its part of the story and at its own level, and you can work on several at once. They're harder than the part they branch from and pay more (crafting materials, Arrowheads, a Requiem Arrow at the end).

5 · The endgame

  • Over Heaven opens with the end of the story: {{ g.tracks|length }} tracks of puzzle fights where a team built for each fight's rule beats raw rarity. Start with {{ g.tracks[0].title }}, which teaches the rules one at a time. ({% for t in g.tracks %}{{ t.title }} · {{ t.fights }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %})
  • Over Heaven co-op raids open at the same time: the weekly raid boss with a rule, for 2 or 3 players.
  • Training ground opens once the story and every Alternate Universe chapter are cleared: level any stand fast and earn chips.

Stuck on a fight?

  • Run it in the team simulator first, or try other stands and gear in the team planner.
  • Level and fuse your team (upgrading stands), equip and refine items, and socket chips.
  • Look for a synergy: a team that shares a crew or a part hits much harder (explorer).
  • Replay cleared stages for stand XP while you wait for a level gate.

Modes

Ranked, seasons & duels

Fight other players turn by turn. Ranked duels move your ELO and your season rating; friendly duels are just for fun.

Ranked matchmaking

Queue from Battle → Ranked to be matched against a player close to your ELO. First pick your ranked roster: 5 different stands from your whole collection. Once someone is found, a pick-and-ban opens: you each ban 2 of the other's stands (20 seconds; run out and their 2 strongest are banned for you), then put your 3 survivors in fighting order (20 seconds; run out and your roster order is used). Then the duel starts. Bring depth, not just your three best. A win adds {{ facts.season_win }} ELO and a loss takes {{ facts.season_loss }} (never below zero). If both teams fall together it's a draw and nobody's ELO moves.

Every pick has a turn clock. When it runs out, your stand picks a target on its own; run out twice in a row and you lose the duel.

Seasons

A ranked season lasts one calendar month. Next to your lifetime ELO you have a season rating that uses the same tiers and moves by the same amounts. A new season doesn't wipe it: you start from half the rating you finished your last season with (in your first season, half your lifetime ELO). See where you stand on Battle → Ranked and on the Season ladder.

Play at least {{ facts.season_min }} ranked duels in a month to qualify. Once the season ends, claim your reward on Battle → Ranked (you get a notification). The reward follows the tier you finished in:

Season titles name the tier and the month, like “Requiem · October 2026”. They're given from {{ facts.season_title_from }} up and stay on your account for good. Show one under your name from your profile (see titles). Unclaimed rewards wait for up to 6 months.

Friendly duels

Challenge anyone by username or Discord ID from Battle → Friends, or with the ⚔ Challenge button on a profile or friend. They have 5 minutes to accept from their inbox. Friendly duels don't change ELO or your season, but they count toward duel quests and achievements.

Every duel can be watched live and replayed afterwards: see replays, live duels & simulator.

For practice without risk, fight the training dummy from the same Battle page.

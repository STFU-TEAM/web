Getting & upgrading stands

Summoning

Stands come from banners. An Arrowhead buys a 10-pull from one banner's pool. Every pull without an SSR or better adds 1 pity, and any SSR or better resets it. At {{ PITY_LIMIT }} pity the next stand is a guaranteed SSR or UR (50/50). Every 10-pull also holds at least one SR.

Earning Arrowheads

  • The daily reward on your team page, every {{ facts.daily_hours|int }} h ({{ facts.daily_hours_donor|int }} h for supporters).
  • Quests and achievements.
  • Buying one for 5,000 Meteor Dust in the shop.

Levels

Stands earn XP from every fight mode. {{ facts.xp_per_level }} XP is one level, up to level {{ facts.max_level }}. Levels raise health, damage, speed and critical. Stands below level {{ facts.catch_up }} learn twice as fast, so a new pull can catch up with your team.

Fusing (awakening)

Fuse a spare copy from storage into another copy of the same stand (the kept one can be in your team). The kept copy takes the other's XP plus a bonus by rarity (R 25, SR 50, SSR 200, UR 400, LR 600 XP). It also gains an awakening, once it has the level for it: ★1 at Lv 20, ★2 at Lv 50, ★3 at Lv 80. Below that level a copy still pays its XP. The consumed copy's items return to your inventory. Each awakening raises bonus stats by another third.

Ascension

A team stand at level {{ facts.max_level }} with fewer than 3 awakenings can ascend: it gains an awakening and its XP resets to 0 so it can level again.

Past ★3, only a Requiem Arrow raises awakening further, up to ★5, the cap for every stand. At level {{ facts.max_level }}, a Requiem Arrow instead evolves Killer Queen (★2), Silver Chariot (★2) and Gold Experience (★3) into Bites the Dust, Chariot Requiem and Gold Experience Requiem.

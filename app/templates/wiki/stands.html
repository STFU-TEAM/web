Getting & upgrading stands

Summoning

Stands come from banners. An Arrowhead buys a 10-pull from one banner's pool. Every pull without a UR or LR adds 1 pity, and only a UR or LR resets it (SSRs don't). At {{ PITY_LIMIT }} pity the next stand is a guaranteed UR, or an LR {{ (PITY_ODDS['LR'] * 100)|round|int }}% of the time. Every 10-pull also holds at least one SR, and your very first 10-pull holds at least one SSR.

🎴 Revealing: R and SR cards turn on their own; an SSR or better waits for your tap. A UR or an LR builds up exactly like an SSR, so you never know until it turns, though sharp eyes may catch a faint hint before it does (only URs and LRs ever show one). Reveal all and Skip turn the rest and leave the SSR+ face down for you. Hovering a face-down card tints it toward its rarity.

✨ Shiny: every stand you get (banners, {{ PALMS }}, the spark shop, Stand Chips) has a 1 in {{ SHINY_ODDS|num }} chance to be shiny, a rare colour variant with a holo frame. It's purely cosmetic, it stays shiny through fusion and Requiem, and a fused copy passes its shine on.

Every 10-pull also earns a spark. With {{ facts.spark_cost }} sparks you can pick any SSR from a banner's pool on its banner page, so a long bad streak still ends with the stand you wanted.

Earning Arrowheads

  • The 7th day of your daily streak, and rarely the daily reward itself (every {{ facts.daily_hours|int }} h, {{ facts.daily_hours_donor|int }} h for supporters).
  • The bosses of Parts 3, 5 and 8 in the story, Alternate Universe bosses and Over Heaven.
  • Every 20th tower floor and the 6th and 12th boss rush bosses, once a week; gang raid tiers.
  • Quests, achievements, Stand Dex sets and ranked seasons.
  • The shop, a few a week.

Levels

Stands earn XP from every fight mode. {{ facts.xp_per_level }} XP is one level, up to level {{ facts.max_level }}. Levels raise health, damage, speed and critical. Stands below level {{ facts.catch_up }} learn twice as fast, so a new pull can catch up with your team.

Fusing

Fuse a spare copy from storage into another copy of the same stand (the kept one can be in your team). The kept copy takes the other's XP plus a bonus by rarity ({% for r, xp in facts.fuse_bonus.items() %}{{ r }} {{ xp|num }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} XP: the rarer the copy, the more it teaches). Fusing gives XP only, never a star. The consumed copy's items return to your inventory. Auto-fuse (R and SR) keeps the copies your stands still need for their stars.

Stand Discs

Releasing a stand gives 💿 Stand Discs: {% for r, v in facts.disc_value.items() %}{{ r }} {{ v }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. A stand with stars gives that much again for every copy its stars used, so a team member you replace isn't wasted. On a stand's panel, Discs buy a fresh copy of it (new types and qualities, level 0, ★0) for its stars: {% for r, v in facts.disc_price.items() %}{{ r }} {{ v|num }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. Only stands a banner sells can be copied, and only ones you own.

Awakening (stars)

A team stand gains a star (an awakening) on its panel, once it has the level for it, by using up spare copies of itself from storage (unlocked, the least advanced first; their XP carries over and their items come back). A Requiem Arrow stands in for one of those copies.

★5 is the cap for every stand, so a stand takes {{ facts.star_copies.values()|sum }} spare copies from ★0 to ★5. Each awakening raises the stand's bonus stats by another {{ facts.star_share }}% of their base (×{{ facts.star5_mult }} at ★5).

Requiem Arrows are rare: the bosses of story Parts 6, 7 and 8 and of the Alternate Universe chapters from Part 5 on give one on their first clear, the shop sells one a week, the Requiem Tower gives one every {{ facts.rq_arrow_every }} floors, the B9 boss of a dungeon sometimes drops one, and the Mirror World very rarely. At {{ facts.max_level }}, a Requiem Arrow can instead evolve Killer Queen (★2+), Silver Chariot (★2+) and Gold Experience (★3+) into Bites the Dust, Chariot Requiem and Gold Experience Requiem. It's your choice each time (an Arrow star or ✧ Requiem, on the stand's panel), so you can keep the original and take it to ★5, and still evolve it later. Evolving starts the Requiem form at level 0 and ★0; items carry over.

Getting & upgrading stands

Summoning

Stands come from banners. An Arrowhead buys a 10-pull from one banner's pool. Every pull without a UR or LR adds 1 pity, and only a UR or LR resets it (SSRs don't). At {{ PITY_LIMIT }} pity the next stand is a guaranteed UR, or an LR {{ (PITY_ODDS['LR'] * 100)|round|int }}% of the time. Every 10-pull also holds at least one SR.

✨ Shiny: every stand you get (banners, {{ PALMS }}, the spark shop, Stand Chips) has a 1 in {{ SHINY_ODDS|num }} chance to be shiny, a rare colour variant with a holo frame. It's purely cosmetic, it stays shiny through fusion and Requiem, and a fused copy passes its shine on.

Every 10-pull also earns a spark. With {{ facts.spark_cost }} sparks you can pick any SSR from a banner's pool on its banner page, so a long bad streak still ends with the stand you wanted.

Earning Arrowheads

  • The 7th day of your daily streak, and rarely the daily reward itself (every {{ facts.daily_hours|int }} h, {{ facts.daily_hours_donor|int }} h for supporters).
  • The bosses of Parts 3, 5 and 8 in the story, Alternate Universe bosses and Over Heaven.
  • Every 20th tower floor and the last boss rush boss, once a week; gang raid tiers.
  • Quests, achievements, Stand Dex sets and ranked seasons.
  • The shop, a few a week.

Levels

Stands earn XP from every fight mode. {{ facts.xp_per_level }} XP is one level, up to level {{ facts.max_level }}. Levels raise health, damage, speed and critical. Stands below level {{ facts.catch_up }} learn twice as fast, so a new pull can catch up with your team.

Fusing (awakening)

Fuse a spare copy from storage into another copy of the same stand (the kept one can be in your team). The kept copy takes the other's XP plus a bonus by rarity ({% for r, xp in facts.fuse_bonus.items() %}{{ r }} {{ xp|num }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} XP: the rarer the copy, the more it teaches). It also gains an awakening, once it has the level for it: ★1 at Lv 20, ★2 at Lv 50, ★3 at Lv 80. Below that level a copy still pays its XP. The consumed copy's items return to your inventory. Each awakening raises bonus stats by another third.

Ascension

A team stand at level {{ facts.max_level }} with fewer than 3 awakenings can ascend: it gains an awakening and its XP resets to 0 so it can level again.

Past ★3, only a Requiem Arrow raises awakening further, up to ★5, the cap for every stand. At level {{ facts.max_level }}, a Requiem Arrow can instead evolve Killer Queen (★2+), Silver Chariot (★2+) and Gold Experience (★3+) into Bites the Dust, Chariot Requiem and Gold Experience Requiem. It's your choice each time (★ Awaken or ✧ Requiem, in your items or on the stand's panel), so you can keep the original and take it to ★5, and still evolve it later. Evolving starts the Requiem form at level 0 and ★0; items carry over.

Basics

Team management

Main team

Your team holds up to 3 stands. They fight in every mode, hold items, and are the only ones you can ascend or reforge. Build around synergies and terrain.

Storage

Everything else waits in storage, up to {{ facts.storage_capacity }} stands. Fuse duplicates there, or release stands you don't need (move them out of the team first).

Managing stands

Tap any stand to open its panel: full stats, types, terrain and synergy, items, and every action (team, fuse, ascend, reforge, release). In storage you can search, filter by rarity, show only fusable duplicates, sort by rarity, level or power, and select several stands to release at once.

Lock your favourites: a locked stand can't be released, consumed by a fusion or offered in a trade until you unlock it.

Trades

Offer stands, items and fragments to another player from Trades. Nothing moves until they accept, and both sides are checked again at that moment. Items equipped on a stand travel with it.

Presets

Save up to {{ facts.max_presets }} presets and swap your whole lineup at once. Loading a preset moves stands between team and storage for you. Released stands drop out of presets automatically.

Specials scale with a stat

Every special scales with one stat, shown on its stand page: ⚔️ damage, 🛡️ armor, ❤️ health, 💨 speed or 🍀 luck (critical). Its power multiplies the special's hits, damage over time, buffs, debuffs and true damage (and heals, for health specials):

  • Build: how far your items and types raise that stat above the stand's natural value. +1 speed is +2% power, +1 critical +1.2%, +10% health +10%. Damage specials don't need it: their hits already use damage. Armor specials grow through their armor buffs instead.
  • Type affinity: the matching type adds {% for q, v in facts.affinity.items() %}{{ '+' if v >= 0 }}{{ (v * 100)|round|int }}% ({{ q|replace('_', ' ')|title }}){{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. BALANCE gives half, for any stat.
  • Power stays between ×{{ facts.power_range[0] }} and ×{{ facts.power_range[1] }}. A speed stand with Speed Universal and Speedwagon's Hat fires its special around ×2; the same stand on Attack Universal hits harder with basic attacks but its special stays at ×1.

So the best roll depends on the stand: a damage stand wants Attack, a healer wants Defense and health items, a sniper wants Luck and critical items. Each stand page tells you which.

Tips

  • A bad first roll guarantees a second type roll.
  • Types stack: Attack Great plus Luck Supreme boosts both damage and critical.
  • Unhappy with a roll? Take any stand to the Forge. A reroll costs {% for r, p in facts.reforge_prices.items() %}{{ p|num }} ({{ r }}){{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} Meteor Dust. Lock the pairs you want to keep (each lock multiplies the price by {{ facts.reforge_lock_mult }}) and compare the old and new rolls before you choose. Nothing changes until you keep the new one.

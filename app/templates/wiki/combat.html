Combat

Combat system

Turn order

Each round, every stand acts in speed order, fastest first. A stand either attacks or, when its special meter is full, fires its special instead. Status effects resolve at the end of each stand's turn, and terrain is re-checked every round.

Stats

  • ❤️HealthAt 0 the stand is out.
  • ⚔️DamageBase power of attacks and most specials.
  • 💨SpeedTurn order and dodging. A faster defender dodges with a chance equal to the speed gap, capped at {{ facts.dodge_cap }}%.
  • 🛡️ArmorDamage taken is multiplied by (armor + 100) ÷ (2 × armor), at most ×10. At 100 armor hits land normally; more armor takes less, down toward half.
  • 🎯CriticalPercent chance to crit. Several specials scale with it.
  • 🪧TauntTank stands draw basic attacks and get +100 health and armor.

Specials

Every stand has a unique special. Its meter fills by 1 each turn and fires automatically when it reaches the stand's charge time, shown on its stand page. Some specials change with terrain or synergies.

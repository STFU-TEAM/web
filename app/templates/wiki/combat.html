Combat

Combat system

Turn order

Teams take turns; the faster team goes first (checked after synergies and ambushes, so slowing a fast team can steal the first move). The team that moves second opens in a counter stance: every special starts one turn closer, +{{ facts.counter_armor }}% armor and +{{ facts.counter_damage }}% damage for its first {{ facts.counter_turns }} turns, and +1 critical for every point of speed it gave up (up to +{{ facts.counter_crit }}) for the whole fight. Speed wins the tempo, not the fight. On your turn every stand attacks once, in team order, and you choose each target. Taunt stands must be hit first. A stand whose special is charged fires it right after its attack, and single-target specials strike the enemy you just picked, so aim your specials. Terrain is re-checked at the start of every turn.

Stats

  • ❤️HealthAt 0 the stand is out. Nothing heals past max health.
  • ⚔️DamageBase power of attacks and most specials. Health and damage grow at the same rate with levels, so fights take about as long at level 1 as at level {{ facts.max_level }}.
  • 💨SpeedDodging and terrain control. A faster defender dodges with a chance of 1% per {{ facts.dodge_per }} points of speed gap, capped at {{ facts.dodge_cap }}%.
  • 🛡️ArmorDamage taken is multiplied by 200 ÷ (100 + armor): 100 armor is neutral, 0 takes double, and the cap of {{ facts.armor_cap }} takes {{ facts.armor_floor }}%.
  • 🎯CriticalPercent chance to crit, up to {{ facts.crit_cap }}%. A crit deals ×{{ facts.crit_multiplier }}.
  • 🪧TauntTank stands draw basic attacks and get +{{ facts.taunt_armor }} armor.

Specials

Every stand has a unique special. Its meter fills by 1 at the end of each of its own turns and fires when it reaches the stand's charge time, shown on its stand page: a charge of 2 fires every other turn. Buffs and debuffs are a share of the stat and last a set number of the affected stand's turns. Permanent growth from specials stops at +{{ facts.growth_cap }}% of the starting stat. Some specials change with terrain or synergies.

Sudden death

From round {{ facts.sudden_death_round }}, every stand loses {{ facts.sudden_death_step }}% of its max health at the end of its turn, {{ facts.sudden_death_step }}% more each round, and heals only {{ facts.sudden_death_heal }}% as much. If both teams still stand after round {{ facts.max_rounds }}, the team with the larger share of its health left wins.

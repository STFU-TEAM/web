Synergy explorer

Put at least two members of a group in your team of three and the synergy wakes up. Fallen teammates still count. Two things happen:

  • Team bonus: every member of the group on your team gets the group's stat bonus for the whole fight. A stand in several active groups gets its biggest group in full, then 60%, then 40% of the others.
  • Special upgrades: the members marked special+ also get a stronger special.

Weaker stands gain more. Each member's team bonus, and its terrain bonus as a native, is multiplied by its rarity: {% for l in leverage %}{{ l.name }} ({{ l.rarity }}) ×{{ l.mult }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last else '.' }}{% endfor %} With 3 members of one group on the team (a full set), that group's bonus is ×1.5 on top. The numbers below are the base, before rarity.

Resonances: crossing synergies

Light two or more synergies on one team and they resonate. Lower rarities sit in more groups (Tarot, the Nine Gods, La Squadra, Morioh...), so a team of commons can light several at once. Resonances are what let a prepared team beat a stack of UR and LR stands.

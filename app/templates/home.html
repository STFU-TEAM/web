How it works

  1. Log in your way

    Create an account with a username and password, or continue with Discord. Install it on your phone to play like an app.

  2. Pull a banner

    An Arrowhead buys ten stands from one JoJo part. Each comes with its own types and qualities, every 10-pull holds an SR or better, and pity guarantees an SSR within {{ PITY_LIMIT }} pulls.

  3. Step into the Mirror World

    Pick targets turn by turn against a reflection of your own team. Winning pays Meteor Dust, experience and sometimes an item.
